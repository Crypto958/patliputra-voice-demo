One receptionist layer, configured for every call route

External enquiries and room-guest requests follow different workflows. AIRA can be deployed through separate numbers or PBX routes so each caller receives the right conversation and the hotel receives the right record.

01

External guest and enquiry calls

Use a dedicated number, forward the hotel’s existing landline, add an IVR option, or route overflow and unanswered calls.

  • Hotel information and directions
  • Room and dining enquiries
  • Events, weddings and group leads
  • Human escalation and callback capture
02

Calls from rooms and internal extensions

Connect a separate hotel PBX extension or SIP route for guests who are already staying at the property.

  • Housekeeping and extra amenities
  • Maintenance and urgent concerns
  • Cab and guest-service requests
  • Room number and priority capture
03

Landline, forwarding and PBX setup

The production route is selected after reviewing the hotel’s telephone provider and PBX capabilities.

  • Dedicated cloud number
  • Existing-number forwarding
  • IVR and overflow routing
  • Room identity when PBX metadata supports it
04

Management follow-up and records

Each call becomes a concise operational record rather than an unstructured conversation.

  • Caller, language and request category
  • Dates, room, group and contact details
  • Priority, escalation and next action
  • Transcript summary and outcome
